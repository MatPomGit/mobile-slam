## Interpretation

The observation supports **functional on-screen FAST-to-KLT visualization**, visible track propagation, replenishment and changing counters. It does not establish per-track geometric correctness, forward–backward residual distributions, actual analyzed CameraX FPS, KLT latency, pure-rotation robustness or successful map initialization. The capture stream may run at a different frame rate from CameraX ImageAnalysis; do not derive camera FPS from the video's H.264 frame rate.
